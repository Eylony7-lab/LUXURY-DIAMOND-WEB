Source of truth for the `sales-partnerships` agent. Read before every task. Update when facts change.

## Product (verified from the product build)
Good Ratio connects diners with nutrition-aware restaurant dishes and gives restaurants tools to manage menus.

**For diners (free side of the marketplace):**
- Set calorie and macro targets (13-question onboarding, Mifflin–St Jeor formula)
- Discover nearby restaurant dishes that fit their daily targets
- Log food; ask an AI coach about their own data; nutrition flags

**For restaurants (paying customer):**
- Upload a menu → AI extracts nutrition data → owner reviews, confirms and publishes dishes
- Analytics from shared menu data and anonymized diner activity
- Plan-based insights, QR menus, integrations
- Paid subscription (Stripe)
- Entry points: pilot application and letter of intent (LOI)

## ICP — who we sell to
- **Target geography:** Miami, Florida (Miami-Dade first)
- **Qualifying rule:** any restaurant that already publishes its nutritional values (on its website, menu, app or in-store)
- **Buyer:** owners and GMs for independents; for groups/chains: marketing, digital or operations leads
- **Two segments inside this rule (they buy for different reasons):**
  1. **Chains with 20+ US locations.** FDA menu labeling makes them publish calories, so nearly all qualify. Their data already exists, so AI extraction is a weak hook. Sell distribution to diners who are actively tracking macros, the verified listing, and analytics. Natural tier: Elite (unlimited locations, API integrations, white-glove onboarding).
  2. **Independents and small groups that publish nutrition voluntarily** (health-forward, fast-casual, bowl/salad/meal-prep concepts). They already care about this audience. Natural tier: Pro (1 location) or Starter.
- **Disqualify:** restaurants with no published nutrition data (keep for a later phase)
